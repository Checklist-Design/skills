# Waitlist — Website

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/waitlist

## Items

### Email capture
A simple form (typically just an email field) to join the waitlist, with a clear statement of what joining means

### What to expect
What waitlist members will receive (early access, a discount, a specific feature) and a rough timeline for when

_Tip: Vague waitlists that promise nothing specific generate sign-ups but very low activation when access eventually opens_

### Social proof
The total number of people already on the waitlist, displayed to signal demand and reduce the fear of backing something niche

### Position indicator
The user's current position in the queue, shown after sign-up and updated as they move up

_Tip: Showing a specific position ('You are #847') is more motivating than a generic confirmation because it creates a concrete reference point to improve from_

### Referral mechanic
A shareable link that moves the user up the list when others join through it, incentivising organic sharing

_Tip: Referral waitlists consistently grow faster than static ones. The mechanic works because it aligns the user's interest with the product's growth_

### Progress toward access
An indication of how close the product is to general launch or how many spots remain at the current access tier
