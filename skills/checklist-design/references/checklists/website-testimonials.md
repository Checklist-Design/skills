# Testimonials — Website

A page dedicated to social proof, collecting customer quotes, reviews, and success stories in one place.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/testimonials

## Items

### Customer quotes
Direct quotes from real customers describing their experience, attributed with name, role, and company

_Tip: Specific quotes that mention a concrete outcome are far more persuasive than generic praise ('It saved us 6 hours a week' beats 'Love this tool')_

### Customer details
Name, role, company, and photo for each testimonial, since anonymised quotes carry much less weight

_Tip: A recognisable company logo next to a quote multiplies its credibility significantly_

### Use case variety
Testimonials representing different customer types, industries, and use cases so visitors can find someone like them

### Video testimonials
Short video clips from customers who are willing to speak on camera, for visitors who find video more convincing

### Review platform badges
Aggregate scores from G2, Trustpilot, Capterra, or similar platforms linking to verified reviews

_Tip: Third-party review scores carry more weight than self-selected quotes, when scores are strong, lead with them_
