# Document View — Web app

A rich content view for reading, editing, and collaborating on structured documents.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/web-app/document-view

## Items

### Document title and metadata
The document's name, owner, and last-modified details visible at the top.

_Tip: An editable inline title is the expected pattern — requiring a separate settings screen to rename a document is a common frustration._

### Table of contents
A navigable outline generated from the document's headings.

_Tip: A table of contents that requires scrolling back to the top to access defeats its own purpose — sticky or floating placement is what makes it useful._

### Rich text with inline media
Support for formatted text, images, embeds, and other content types within the body.

_Tip: Most users bring content from Word or Google Docs — how gracefully paste handles those imports determines how much cleanup work they face._

### View and edit mode distinction
A clear difference between reading a document and actively editing it.

_Tip: Accidental edits are a major frustration in collaborative documents — a clear mode switch prevents content being changed unintentionally._

### Version history or change tracking
A log of previous versions and the ability to restore or compare them.

_Tip: Even basic 'last 10 versions' history is enormously valuable — it is one of the most-cited reasons users trust a platform with important documents._

### Auto-save and last-saved indicator
Changes save automatically, with a visible timestamp of when the last save occurred.

_Tip: 'Saving...' → 'Saved 3 seconds ago' is the gold standard — it reassures users without interrupting them._

### Inline comments or annotations
The ability to add comments to specific sections or selections of text.

_Tip: Flat unthreaded comments pile up quickly in a collaborative document — threading keeps conversations self-contained and the document readable._

### Share and permission controls
A way to share the document and control whether recipients can view or edit.

_Tip: A document shared with an entire organisation looks identical to a private one without a persistent sharing state indicator — that ambiguity causes accidental data exposure._
