# Affiliate — Website

A page that invites potential affiliates or partners to join a programme, explaining how it works and what they stand to earn.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/affiliate

## Items

### Programme overview
A clear explanation of what the affiliate or partner programme involves and who it is designed for

### Commission or reward structure
Exactly what affiliates earn (percentage, flat fee, recurring commission) stated plainly

_Tip: Ambiguous commission structures are the most common reason potential affiliates leave this page without applying_

### How it works
A simple step-by-step breakdown of joining, sharing, and earning

_Tip: If using a third party to connect, it's helpful to include screenshots for steps_

### Resources provided
What materials the company provides (links, banners, copy, tracking) to help affiliates succeed

### Social proof
Testimonials or earnings examples from existing affiliates who are benefiting from the programme

### Application or signup CTA
A clear form or button to join the programme
