# Team — Website

A team page introduces an organization's staff members, leadership, or key personnel. It typically helps visitors understand the people behind the company while adding a human element to the brand's identity.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/team

## Items

### Personality and branding
A theme or tone that conveys how serious or funny your team behaves

_Tip: Colloquial copy helps you appears more accessible to users_

### Team members
A representation of your team, with amount of detail varying on the team size (8 people can be shown individually, but 800 not so much)

### Visuals
Either photography or illustrations of the team, offices or culture

### Group by area or team
Depending on your team size, you could divide the team members up by area (marketing, design, engineering) to allow an easier scan of members

### Link to careers
After someone has seen who your team is and the culture, they may be interested in finding what job opportunities are available
