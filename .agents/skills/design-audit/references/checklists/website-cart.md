# Cart — Website

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/cart

## Items

### Item name and image
Each item shown with its name and image, matching what was displayed on the product page.

### Update or remove item
The ability to adjust quantity, change variants, or remove an item directly from the cart.

### Item variations
Selected variants for each item — size, colour, style, or type — displayed clearly alongside the item.

_Tip: A mismatch between what the user selected and what appears in the cart is a common source of disputes — the variant should always be clearly visible._

### Promo or discount code
A text field for entering a promo, discount, or coupon code.

_Tip: Showing whether the code was accepted or rejected immediately after entry removes any ambiguity._

### Accepted payment methods
The payment methods available for completing the purchase — cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and others.

_Tip: Displaying recognised payment method logos increases trust at the most anxious point of the purchase flow._

### Link to support
A way to get help with items in the cart — particularly useful for products with configurable options or custom orders.

### Checkout button
A prominent button to proceed to payment when the user is ready to complete their order.

### Suggested add-ons
Related or complementary items suggested based on what is already in the cart.
