# Blog — Website

A blog page aggregates and displays multiple articles or posts in a chronological order, typically showing previews, titles, publication dates, and categories. It provides easy navigation through pagination or infinite scroll, allowing users to browse and discover content.

Source: https://www.checklist.design/website/blog

## Items

### Hero
A clear indicator it's a blog, whether it's for an individual, team or company

### Navigation
A logo, links to visit, a CTA, and potentially a search function

_Tip: Utilise navigational links to show sub topics_

### Layout
How sections and blog posts are structured

_Tip: Consider what's above the fold on mobile, tablet and desktop Consider blog article order (newest first, trending) A grid layout with multiple posts on a row has more density_

### Blog post preview
The visuals and text of a blog post

_Tip: Typography hierarchy is important here - a user must clearly see the heading first A branded thumbnail sets the mood of the content If you include a description, it should be economical in clearly explaining the blog post_

### Description
Summarise what's covered in the blog — shown in the hero

### Tags and/or categories
A topic that can be attached to a blog post for convenient filtering

### Search function
Have lots of posts? Search makes it easier for the user to find the content they desire

### Opt-in for subscription
A signup form for users to complete to make them a subscriber of the blog's content

### Sidebar
This can contain additional links and resources that support the blog posts e.g. search function, popular posts, trending topics

### Pagination
Break up your content into pages after 10-20 items (there are pros and cons on both sides of the pagination vs infinite scroll argument, so go with what suits your product better!)
